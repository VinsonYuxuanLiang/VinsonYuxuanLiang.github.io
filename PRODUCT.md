# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Hiring managers, recruiters, and data professionals evaluating Vinson Liang for data analyst roles. They spend 30–90 seconds on a portfolio before deciding whether to call for an interview.

Secondary: Peers and collaborators wanting to understand his analytical approach.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio website that converts visitors into interview requests by demonstrating quantitative rigor, communication clarity, and real project depth — not just listing skills.

## Positioning

A math-and-stats-trained analyst who doesn't just build models but translates what they mean into business decisions. The unique mechanism: every project shows the thinking behind the analysis, not just the output.

## Operating Context

- Recruiter reads on laptop during work hours; mobile on commute
- GitHub links are primary proof — they must work
- PDF resume download is a concrete next step
- The site lives at a custom domain (not yet deployed); currently viewed locally

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single HTML file with inline CSS/JS (no build step)
- Real project images in `imgs/` directory
- Contact form uses mailto (no backend)
- No framework dependency
- Must work without JavaScript for basic content access
- Existing color system: indigo (#4F46E5) + sky (#0EA5E9) on light/dark

## Product Principles

1. **Show the work, not just the result.** Every project card hints at the analytical thinking behind it.
2. **Speed over decoration.** Fast load, instant readability, zero friction to contact.
3. **Authority through specificity.** Numbers, tools, and outcomes — never vague claims.
